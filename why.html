Why we started ShowTend

Good work happens every day.

Most of it never makes it online.

A nail artist finishes the last set at 8 p.m. A handyman wraps up a job across town. A trainer has been on since 6 a.m.

The work is great. Posting can wait — and it usually does.

Meanwhile, new clients are scrolling, deciding who to call.

You really have two shops: the one where you do the work, and the one people see when they look you up.

The second one usually gets whatever time is left.

So that's what ShowTend is for:

Making good work easy to find.

Without turning you into a full-time content creator.
